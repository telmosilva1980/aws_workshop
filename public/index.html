Ship & Deliver

Run cargo across open water. Dodge the sea. Beat the dock.

How to play

  • Move: WASD / Arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick.
  • Boost: Space, or the BOOST button.
  • Collect glowing cargo, then deliver it to the port before time runs out.
  • Deliver quickly in succession to build a combo multiplier.
  • Avoid icebergs, rocks, storm swells and the pirate ship — a hit costs cargo and time.
